DEFINITION

What is Click Image Zoom?

Click Image Zoom is a Chrome extension that lets you open webpage images in an in-page viewer, zoom up to 10x, and apply optional local 4x AI enhancement without uploading image pixels to a cloud AI service.

Three moves. More detail.

No hover popups and no download-then-open detour. Your browsing flow stays uninterrupted.

HOLD

Choose Shift, Alt, Ctrl, or Command as your trigger key.

CLICK

Click a product image, screenshot, chart, or thumbnail on any webpage.

INSPECT

Zoom with the wheel, drag to pan, and generate a 4x enhanced version when needed.

Small images deserve a closer look.

Local Real-ESRGAN gives you two processing modes. If it is unavailable, the viewer falls back gracefully and keeps working.

  • Choose speed or image quality
  • Run on open or on first zoom
  • Set a maximum AI processing size

Built for fast visual checks.

Inspect product images, article screenshots, charts, avatars, and image search results directly on the page.

FAQ

Common questions

Are images uploaded for AI enhancement?

No. Enhancement runs locally in the browser and on your device.

Does the extension require an account?

No account is required for the documented core workflow.

What happens when AI is unavailable?

The viewer, zoom, pan, and standard clarity mode remain available.
